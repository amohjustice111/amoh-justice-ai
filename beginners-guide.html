STEP 01 · YOUR DASHBOARD

Find your workspace

Open Dashboard to see the main sections. Use the left navigation to move between your tools without reloading the whole dashboard.

Go to Dashboard ↗
STEP 02 · AI CREATOR STUDIO

Start a creative project

Open AI Creator Studio to work with image and video prompts. Check each tool’s availability before expecting a generation to run.

Open AI Studio ↗
STEP 03 · YOUR PROJECTS

Review your work

Open My Projects or the gallery to review creations and saved work. Saving and syncing depend on the tool and your signed-in account setup.

Open Project Gallery ↗
STEP 04 · LEARN

Build your skills

Use AI Academy and the website tutorials to learn prompting, design, video creation, and other digital skills step by step.

Open AI Academy ↗
STEP 05 · ACCOUNT

Sign in and manage your account

Use Account & Sign in for account access. Keep your password and private login details to yourself; support will never need them.

Open Account ↗
STEP 06 · HELP

Get support when needed

Open Community & Help for preferences, common answers, and WhatsApp support. Describe the page and error you see, but never send passwords or secret keys.

Open Help & Settings ↗
