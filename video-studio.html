RECOMMENDED VIDEO LENGTH & FREE-FRIENDLY SETTINGS

Choose 6 seconds for the recommended starting length, or 8 seconds when you want a longer clip. Set Height: 576, Width: 1024, and choose the Conv decoder if it is offered. These lower-resolution settings may reduce GPU use, but longer videos need more processing and are not guaranteed to fit the free shared GPU allowance.

If 6 or 8 seconds triggers a GPU-duration error, try 6 seconds first, lower the dimensions if the demo allows it, or retry later. Do not pay for PRO just to use this page.

Free access and important limits
  • AMOH JUSTICE AI does not charge you or spend AMOH credits for this embedded generator.
  • The public demo sends generation requests from your browser to the Lightricks LTX-2.5 Hugging Face Space. AMOH's server does not process your prompt or uploaded image in this workflow. The recommended 1024×576, 6-second (or optional 8-second), Conv settings are a lower-demand starting point for those durations; if the Space rejects the reservation, its free GPU allowance is currently insufficient.
  • Visitors share a public GPU queue. It can be busy, slow, or temporarily return “no GPU available” or “quota exceeded”. Retrying later may help; unlimited or instant generation cannot be promised.
  • The demo may offer an optional Hugging Face access token for its own queue limits. It is not required to try anonymous access. If you choose to enter one, the demo says it stores it in this browser and sends it directly to the Lightricks Space; never paste it into AMOH chat or share it with anyone.
  • Only upload images you have permission to use. Avoid private or sensitive photos. The provider controls its service, queue, data handling and availability.
